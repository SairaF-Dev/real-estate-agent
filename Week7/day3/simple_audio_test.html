Troubleshooting

If no audio plays:
  1. Check your system volume is not muted
  2. Check browser volume is not muted (speaker icon in tab)
  3. Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
  4. Open F12 console and look for red errors
  5. Check if your audio device is working
